SFRC Corbel Shear Capacity Calculator

Predicts the shear capacity (Vexp) of stirrup-free steel-fiber-reinforced concrete corbels from Linear Regression, Ridge Regression, and XGBoost models trained on a 108-specimen, seven-source-study database.
Scope of applicability. Predictions are decision-support estimates valid strictly within the parameter ranges of the compiled database (shown as hints below). The model does not represent absolute effective depth (d) or section height (h) — only a/d — and section width (b) spans only 150–156 mm across the database. Do not use for corbels with absolute dimensions, section widths, or material properties outside these ranges. See Table 2 and Section 4 of the source paper.
